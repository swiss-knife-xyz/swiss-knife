## Evidence boundaries

Roundup publication dates are not shutdown dates. A deadline for withdrawals, migration, refunds or frontend access is not automatically the date a blockchain stops. The archive exposes announcement dates and service-end dates separately. Exact dates remain absent when unsupported.

Original websites can be dead, repurposed or rebranded. Historical links are identity/source references, not instructions to connect wallets or transact. Secondary evidence is visibly labeled, and exclusions or uncertain candidates are retained in the research notes.
